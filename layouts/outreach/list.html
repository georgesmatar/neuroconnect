Global Health Missions

Our team brings surgical expertise to underserved communities internationally, while building local capacity through education and mentorship.

🇭🇹
Neurosurgery Mission — Haiti
February 2017 & March 2018

Dr. Weil led two neurosurgical missions to Haiti with a team from CHU Sainte-Justine and CHUM. The team performed operations, ran outpatient clinics, and delivered teaching sessions at the Université Notre-Dame d'Haïti for Haitian medical students and residents.

  • Surgeries performed in under-resourced operating theatres
  • Lectures on epilepsy surgery, traumatic brain injury management, and pediatric neurosurgery
  • Supervised Montreal residents (R5–R6) alongside Haitian trainees
🇭🇹
Epilepsy Clinic Mission — Haiti (CLIDEP)
2018

A dedicated neurology and epilepsy care mission to the CLIDEP epilepsy clinics in Haiti, co-organized with the CHUM neurology team. The mission provided diagnosis, treatment, and clinical teaching to Haitian healthcare providers.

  • Epilepsy clinic consultations for patients with limited access to specialty care
  • Teaching on seizure management, EEG interpretation, and surgical candidacy
🇺🇦
Child Neurologists Unite for Ukraine
2022

Dr. Hadjinicolaou co-organized an international initiative connecting child neurologists with Ukrainian colleagues displaced by the conflict, providing clinical guidance, teleconsultation, and professional support networks.

Education & Teaching

Formal Courses

  • MMD-1232 — Exploration du système nerveux (1st-year MD students, UdeM, 2015–2018)
  • MMD-35412H — Neurosurgery elective (3rd & 4th-year MD, CHUSJ, since 2016)
  • Pediatric neurosurgery curriculum for neurosurgery residents, UdeM (2016–2019)

Fellowship Program

Dr. Weil is Director of the Pediatric Neurosurgery Fellowship Program at CHU Sainte-Justine (since 2016) — training the next generation of pediatric neurosurgeons from Canada and internationally.

Video-EEG Modules

Dr. Hadjinicolaou co-created Roadmap to EEGs — a series of video-based e-learning modules on clinical EEG reading for trainees worldwide, published in Epileptic Disorders (2022).

Weekly Lab Meetings

The lab holds weekly research meetings (88+ hours/year) where students present results, discuss literature, and receive mentorship — open to all trainees across levels.
