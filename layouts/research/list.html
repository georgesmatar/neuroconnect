Focus Areas

What We Study

Our research spans the translational continuum from neural mechanisms to large-scale clinical outcomes.

01

SEEG & Epilepsy Surgery

Using stereoelectroencephalography, we record directly from deep brain structures to delineate seizure onset zones with millimeter precision. We study high-frequency oscillations, inter-ictal biomarkers, and functional connectivity to guide surgical decisions in drug-resistant focal epilepsy.

  • SEEG implantation & VR surgical planning (PreVISE)
  • Hemispherectomy & hemispherotomy
  • Laser interstitial thermal therapy (MRgLITT)
  • Responsive neurostimulation (RNS)
  • Corpus callosotomy & vagus nerve stimulation
02

Seizure Network Dynamics

We characterize how epileptic networks form, sustain, and propagate seizures using intracranial EEG, diffusion MRI tractography, and graph-theoretic connectivity analysis. Collaborations with the Bernhardt Lab (McGill) and Ibrahim Lab (SickKids) drive large-scale connectome studies.

  • High-frequency oscillation (HFO) analysis
  • Functional & structural connectivity mapping
  • Sinks/sources as seizure propagation biomarkers
  • Virtual Epileptic Patient (VEP) cohort modeling
03

Machine Learning & AI

We develop and validate clinical prediction models and deep learning tools applied to epilepsy care — from predicting surgical outcomes before the operation to automating lesion detection on MRI.

  • HOPS: Hemispherectomy Outcome Prediction Scale (1000+ patients, 30+ centers)
  • Deep learning for VNS response prediction (Nature Communications 2026)
  • MELD: Automated focal cortical dysplasia detection
  • Early drug-resistant epilepsy prediction (CIHR-DRE project)
04

Brain Mapping & Eloquent Cortex

Preserving function while curing epilepsy. We map eloquent cortex using cortical stimulation, fMRI, TMS, and tractography to guide resections near language, motor, and memory areas. Studies include hippocampal memory mapping and perirolandic motor preservation.

  • Intraoperative ECoG & cortical stimulation
  • TMS-hippocampus memory localization (SHM project)
  • Tractography-guided laser ablation for visual preservation
  • Callosotomy EEG interhemispheric connectivity
05

Neuromodulation & DBS

Beyond resection: we study deep brain stimulation for epilepsy and disorders of consciousness, responsive neurostimulation, and vagus nerve stimulation. Our CHILD-DBS multicenter registry is the largest prospective cohort of pediatric DBS outcomes worldwide.

  • Central thalamic DBS for disorders of consciousness
  • Centromedian DBS for drug-resistant epilepsy
  • CONNECTiVOS: VNS biomarker study (10+ centers)
  • Local field potential recording pipeline (open-source)
06

Pediatric Neurosurgery

Our clinical research encompasses the full pediatric neurosurgical spectrum: brain tumors, vascular malformations, spasticity surgery, and global neurosurgery. We lead and participate in multicenter meta-analyses to generate the highest level of evidence for rare pediatric conditions.

  • Pediatric brain tumor molecular profiling & surgery (BRAIN-MAP)
  • Selective dorsal rhizotomy & intrathecal baclofen
  • Neurogenic stunned myocardium meta-analysis (NSM)
  • Global neurosurgery access studies (Haiti)
