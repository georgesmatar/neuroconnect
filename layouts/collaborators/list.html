International Research Consortia

Our lab is a founding and active member of three multinational research networks, each studying different aspects of epilepsy surgery and neuromodulation outcomes.

Active
CONNECTiVOS
Canadian and North American Outcomes Network for Children With Treatment-Resistant Epilepsy Via Vagus nerve stimulation Or other Stimulation therapies

A prospective, multinational study of vagus nerve stimulation (VNS) and deep brain stimulation outcomes in children with drug-resistant epilepsy. Dr. Weil and Dr. Hadjinicolaou are co-investigators. Key publications in Nature Communications (2026) and Epilepsia (2025).

15+ sites North America VNS · DBS · RNS
Active
MELD Project
Multi-centre Epilepsy Lesion Detection

An international collaboration developing automated MRI analysis tools to detect focal cortical dysplasia and other lesions in epilepsy patients. The team contributed to the automated segmentation of post-surgical resection cavities study published in 2025.

30+ sites Europe · Americas · Asia MRI · AI
Active
CHILD-DBS Registry
Canadian and International Registry for Deep Brain Stimulation in Children

A multicenter registry tracking outcomes of deep brain stimulation in pediatric populations across indications including epilepsy, movement disorders, and disorders of consciousness. Multiple publications in Epilepsia and Neurology (2025).

8+ sites Canada · USA DBS · neuromodulation
