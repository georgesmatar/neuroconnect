CONNECTiVOS: Biomarkers of Vagus Nerve Stimulation Response

A prospective multicenter study identifying connectomic and clinical predictors of seizure response to VNS in children with drug-resistant epilepsy. Combines diffusion MRI tractography with clinical outcome measures across 10+ Canadian centers.

Lead: Dr. Faisal Niazi, Dr. Alexander Weil
Students: Nicholas Katsis, Naomie Lussier
Funding: CIHR
VNS Tractography Epilepsy Multicenter
● Active

SEEG & Seizure Network Dynamics in Focal Epilepsy

Using stereo-EEG recordings from patients undergoing pre-surgical evaluation, we characterize high-frequency oscillations, inter-ictal discharges, and functional connectivity to better delineate seizure onset zones and predict surgical outcomes.

Lead: Dr. Aris Hadjinicolaou, Dr. Weil
Students: Xavier Carmona, David Bergeron
Funding: CHU Sainte-Justine Foundation
SEEG HFOs Brain Networks Machine Learning
● Active

Central Thalamic DBS for Disorders of Consciousness

An individual participant data meta-analysis and prospective study evaluating the efficacy of central thalamic deep brain stimulation for promoting arousal and motor recovery in patients with disorders of consciousness following acquired brain injury.

Lead: David Bergeron (PhD)
Supervisor: Dr. Alexander Weil
Status: Data collection ongoing
DBS Consciousness Meta-analysis Neuromodulation

MELD: Automated MRI Lesion Detection for Focal Cortical Dysplasia

We are part of the international MELD consortium developing AI-powered tools for the automated detection and segmentation of focal cortical dysplasia and post-surgical resection cavities on MRI. This project directly improves surgical planning for MRI-negative patients.

Lead: Dr. Alexander Weil (local PI)
Students: Emma Lacoume, Jeanne Fredette
Consortium: MELD (30+ international centers)
AI / Deep Learning MRI FCD International

Hemispherectomy Outcome Prediction Scale (HOPS) — Online Calculator

Building on the validated HOPS prediction tool (Weil et al., Epilepsia 2021, 1000+ patients from 30+ international centers), this project develops an open-access web calculator for predicting seizure freedom after hemispherectomy in children.

Lead: Dr. Alexander Weil
Students: Nicolas Fortier-Lebel
Published: Epilepsia 2024
Prediction Model Hemispherectomy Pediatric Epilepsy Open Access
◉ Ongoing

PreVISE: Virtual Reality SEEG Surgical Planning

Development and clinical validation of PreVISE, an immersive VR system for planning SEEG electrode trajectories in epilepsy surgery. The system enables neurosurgeons to visualize 3D MRI and brain atlases to optimize implantation strategies while avoiding eloquent cortex and vasculature.

Lead: Dr. Weil, Dr. Xiao (CHUSJ)
Students: Naomie Lussier, Nicolas Fortier-Lebel
Published: Virtual Reality 2024
VR / XR SEEG Surgical Planning 3D Visualization
◉ Ongoing

Intraoperative EEG Neuronavigation for Precision Laser Ablation

Combining real-time iEEG with tractography-guided LITT (laser interstitial thermal therapy), this project aims to maximize seizure freedom while preserving eloquent white matter tracts. Includes a focus on visual field preservation in temporal and occipital lobe epilepsy cases.

Lead: Dr. Weil, Dr. Niazi
Students: Xavier Carmona, Emma Lacoume
Funding: Internal Grant
LITT iEEG Tractography Laser Ablation
◉ Ongoing

Long-Term Cognitive Outcomes After Pediatric Epilepsy Surgery

A CIHR-funded prospective study evaluating cognitive, behavioral, and quality-of-life outcomes over 5+ years following epilepsy surgery in children and adolescents. Includes neuropsychological testing, patient-reported outcomes, and neuroimaging biomarkers.

Lead: Dr. Alexander Weil
Students: Naomie Lussier, Jeanne Fredette
Funding: CIHR (3-year grant)
Cognition QoL Prospective Study CIHR
● Completed

MRgLITT for Drug-Resistant Epilepsy — Systematic Review & Meta-Analysis

A comprehensive systematic review and individual patient data meta-analysis of MR-guided laser interstitial thermal therapy (MRgLITT) outcomes in drug-resistant epilepsy, synthesizing data from 50+ studies and 1000+ patients across multiple epilepsy etiologies.

Lead: Dr. Faisal Niazi, Dr. Weil
Published: Epilepsia 2023
MRgLITT Systematic Review Meta-analysis
