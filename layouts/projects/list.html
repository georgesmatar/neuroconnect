Spread — MEM Source Localization on sEEG

Validation of Maximum Entropy on the Mean (MEM) source localization on sEEG data, using patient-specific structural connectivity and graph theory to distinguish surgically relevant from non-relevant regions of seizure spread (srROSS vs nsrROSS).

Supervisor: Dr. Weil, Dr. Hadjinicolaou
Students: Xavier Carmona, Duaa Ali Cheema, Cédérick Montplaisir
