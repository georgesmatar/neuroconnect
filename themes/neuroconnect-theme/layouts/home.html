Our Approach

Precision Surgery for Drug-Resistant Epilepsy

We use Stereo-EEG (SEEG) to implant depth electrodes and record directly from the seizure onset zone. Combined with high-resolution MRI, PET, and machine learning, we identify surgical targets with millimeter precision.

  • SEEG & intracranial EEG recording
  • Laser interstitial thermal therapy (LITT)
  • Responsive neurostimulation (RNS)
  • Computational seizure network modeling

Community Outreach

Haiti Neurosurgery & Epilepsy Missions

Dr. Weil led surgical and epilepsy missions to Haiti (2017–2018) with CHU Sainte-Justine and CHUM teams — operating, running clinics, and teaching Haitian residents at the Université Notre-Dame d'Haïti.

📺

Media & Public Engagement

Featured on Tout le monde en parle (Radio-Canada), La Presse, Le Devoir, and Radio-Canada Première. Patient stories have reached hundreds of thousands of Quebecers.

🎓

Education & Teaching

Dr. Weil directs the Pediatric Neurosurgery Fellowship Program at CHUSJ (since 2016) and teaches formal UdeM courses. 15,000+ teaching hours since 2020 across clinical, academic, and international settings.

🇺🇦

Child Neurologists Unite for Ukraine

Dr. Hadjinicolaou co-organized an initiative connecting child neurologists with Ukrainian colleagues displaced by the conflict, providing teleconsultation and professional support.
