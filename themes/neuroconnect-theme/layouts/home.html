Our Approach

Precision Surgery for Drug-Resistant Epilepsy

We use Stereo-EEG (SEEG) to implant depth electrodes and record directly from the seizure onset zone. Combined with high-resolution MRI, PET, and machine learning, we identify surgical targets with millimeter precision.

  • SEEG & intracranial EEG recording
  • Laser interstitial thermal therapy (LITT)
  • Responsive neurostimulation (RNS)
  • Computational seizure network modeling
