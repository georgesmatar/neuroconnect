## Our Research Mission

The NeuroConnect Lab, led by **Dr. Alexander Weil** at CHU Sainte-Justine and Université de Montréal, focuses on understanding and treating drug-resistant epilepsy through a continuum from molecular mechanisms to surgical outcomes.

We combine state-of-the-art intracranial electrophysiology, multimodal neuroimaging, machine learning, and precision surgical techniques to map, predict, and disrupt epileptic networks.

## Research Areas

### Epilepsy Surgery & SEEG

Stereoelectroencephalography (SEEG) allows us to record directly from deep brain structures to delineate the seizure onset zone with millimeter precision. We study connectivity patterns, high-frequency oscillations, and inter-ictal biomarkers to guide surgical decisions.

**Key approaches:**
- SEEG implantation planning using VR (PreVISE system)
- Laser interstitial thermal therapy (LITT/MRgLITT)
- Hemispherectomy and hemispherotomy
- Responsive neurostimulation (RNS)
- Corpus callosotomy and vagus nerve stimulation (VNS)

### Seizure Network Modeling

Using intracranial EEG and advanced computational models, we characterize the dynamics of epileptic networks — how seizures start, spread, and terminate. We collaborate with leading groups (Bernhardt Lab at McGill, Ibrahim Lab at SickKids) to develop personalized biomarkers for surgical outcomes.

### Machine Learning & AI in Epilepsy

We develop and validate machine learning models for:
- Predicting seizure freedom after surgery (HOPS score — validated in 1000+ patients)
- Automated MRI lesion detection (MELD collaboration)
- VNS response prediction (CONNECTiVOS database)
- Connectome-wide signatures of temporal lobe epilepsy

### Pediatric Neurosurgery

Our clinical research spans the full spectrum of pediatric neurosurgical conditions — from focal cortical dysplasia and hypothalamic hamartoma to brain tumors and deep brain stimulation for movement disorders. We emphasize long-term cognitive and quality-of-life outcomes.
