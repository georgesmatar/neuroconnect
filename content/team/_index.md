We are a multidisciplinary team working at the intersection of clinical medicine and computational neuroscience. Lab members span all career stages — from summer research interns to post-doctoral fellows — and benefit from an environment of close mentorship, weekly lab meetings, and direct clinical exposure at CHU Sainte-Justine.

**Principal Investigators** lead clinical care and research at CHUSJ and CHUM while directing independent research programs. **Post-doctoral fellows** and **PhD students** drive the lab's core scientific projects with dedicated funding. **MSc students** develop focused projects bridging clinical data and engineering methods. **MD students and research interns** gain hands-on exposure to translational research, with many going on to graduate training.

Interested in joining? See the [Contact](/contact/) page for open positions.
