Dr. Aristides Hadjinicolaou is a pediatric epileptologist and co-investigator of the NeuroConnect Lab. He trained at Université de Montréal, McGill, the University of Toronto, and completed a fellowship in Epilepsy and Neurophysiology at Boston Children's Hospital, Harvard Medical School.

He holds dual board certifications in Pediatrics and Neurology (RCPSC, ABP, ABPN) and serves as Associate Clinical Professor at Université de Montréal and Director of the pediatric epilepsy fellowship program. His expertise spans SEEG, cortical stimulation, and surgical treatment of drug-resistant epilepsy. He is a co-investigator on major funded projects including a CIHR neuroimaging study and an IMAGINE Partnership AI initiative, and has co-authored over 30 peer-reviewed publications with the NeuroConnect Lab.
