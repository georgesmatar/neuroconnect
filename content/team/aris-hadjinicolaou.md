Dr. Hadjinicolaou is a pediatric epileptologist and key collaborator of the NeuroConnect Lab. He specializes in the pre-surgical evaluation of children with drug-resistant epilepsy, including SEEG interpretation, cortical stimulation for brain mapping, and intraoperative electrocorticography.

He has co-authored numerous publications with the lab on VNS outcomes, SEEG methodology, and brain mapping practices across Canada.
