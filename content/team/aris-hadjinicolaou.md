Dr. Aristides Hadjinicolaou is a pediatric epileptologist and co-investigator of the NeuroConnect Lab. He completed his MD at Université de Montréal, residencies in Psychiatry at McGill, Pediatrics at McGill, and Pediatric Neurology at the University of Toronto, followed by a clinical fellowship in Epilepsy and Neurophysiology at Boston Children's Hospital, Harvard Medical School.

He holds dual board certifications in Pediatrics (RCPSC, ABP) and Neurology (RCPSC, ABPN), and is a Diplomate of the Canadian Society of Clinical Neurophysiologists (CSCN). Since 2022 he has been an Associate Clinical Professor in the Department of Neurosciences at Université de Montréal and an Associate Researcher at the CHU Sainte-Justine Research Centre (Brain and Child Development). He also serves as Director of the pediatric epilepsy fellowship program at Université de Montréal and Director of the ECHO CHU Sainte-Justine Epilepsie Pédiatrique program.

His clinical and research expertise spans SEEG interpretation and implantation, cortical stimulation for brain mapping, intraoperative electrocorticography, infantile spasms, hypothalamic hamartoma surgery, and emergency neurosurgery for refractory status epilepticus. He is a co-investigator on several funded projects including a CIHR-supported multicenter neuroimaging study ($1,078,650; 2025-2030) and an IMAGINE Partnership project on AI-based brain data exploration ($4,700,000; 2024-2027).

He has co-authored over 30 peer-reviewed publications with the NeuroConnect Lab on VNS outcomes, SEEG methodology, deep brain stimulation, cortical stimulation practices, and surgical outcomes in drug-resistant pediatric epilepsy.
