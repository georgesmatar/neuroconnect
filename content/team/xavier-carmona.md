Xavier is a Master's student in the NeuroConnect Lab working on surgical outcomes and network analysis in insular and perisylvian epilepsy. He contributes to SEEG connectivity studies and is involved in the lab's tractography-guided laser ablation project. He has contributed to published work on surgical outcomes in operculo-insular epilepsy.
