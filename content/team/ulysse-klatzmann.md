Dr. Ulysse Klatzmann is a postdoctoral researcher in the NeuroConnect Lab working on the modeling of large-scale cortical dynamics. He holds a PhD in cognitive neuroscience (computational and theoretical neuroscience) from Université Paris Cité / INCC, a MSc from the CogMaster at École normale supérieure, and an engineering degree from Télécom Paris, with pre-doctoral training at the Center for Neural Science, NYU.

His doctoral work investigated macro-scale gradients of cortical functional organization, introducing Spatial Component Decomposition — a sparse dictionary learning framework on geodesic manifolds — applied to five large-scale fMRI datasets spanning three modalities and over 400 participants. He has also modeled cortex-wide dynamics of conscious access in large-scale spiking networks, published in *Cell Reports* (2025), and studied how cortical architecture emerges in artificial neural networks embedded on cortical geometry during a postdoctoral fellowship at Freie Universität Berlin.

Key research interests: whole-brain modeling, functional connectivity and cortical gradients, neuroimaging analysis, deep learning, and the application of computational and AI methods to epilepsy imaging.
