Raphael Hitti is a research intern with strong experience in systematic reviews, meta-analyses, and clinical data extraction. He works on multiple active projects including the ASM Cognition systematic review, FCD semiology study (in collaboration with Harvard), Grade Newcastle PMG review, and the NEST cortical malformations project. He also mentors junior research volunteers in data extraction methodology.
