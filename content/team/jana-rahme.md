Jana Rahme is a research intern contributing to the Banque EEG infrastructure project, managing the REDCap clinical database for epilepsy patients at CHUSJ. She has prior experience with REDCap, Quanum, Covidence, and neuropsychological research, and also contributes to the NEST cortical malformations project.
