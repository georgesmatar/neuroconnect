Dr. Alexander Weil is a pediatric neurosurgeon and clinician-scientist at CHU Sainte-Justine and Associate Professor at Université de Montréal. He completed his neurosurgical training followed by a fellowship in pediatric epilepsy surgery and leads the NeuroConnect Lab with a focus on the surgical treatment of drug-resistant epilepsy in children and adults.

His research spans the full translational spectrum — from molecular mechanisms of seizurogenesis to large multicenter outcome studies and clinical prediction tools used worldwide. He has authored over 124 peer-reviewed publications and serves as an investigator in major international consortia including CONNECTiVOS, MELD, and CHILD-DBS.

Key research interests: SEEG, laser interstitial thermal therapy (LITT), hemispherectomy, machine learning for surgical outcome prediction, deep brain stimulation, and neuromodulation for epilepsy.
