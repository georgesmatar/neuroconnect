Dr. Alexander Weil is a pediatric neurosurgeon and clinician-scientist at CHU Sainte-Justine and Associate Professor at Université de Montréal. He leads the NeuroConnect Lab with a focus on surgical treatment of drug-resistant epilepsy in children and adults.

Dr. Weil completed his neurosurgical training followed by a fellowship in pediatric epilepsy surgery. His research spans the full translational spectrum — from molecular mechanisms of seizurogenesis to large multicenter outcome studies and the development of clinical prediction tools used worldwide.

**Research Interests:** SEEG, laser ablation, hemispherectomy, machine learning for surgical outcome prediction, brain stiffness, deep brain stimulation.

**Selected Recognition:** 124+ peer-reviewed publications, CIHR-funded investigator, contributor to the CONNECTiVOS, MELD, and CHILD-DBS multicenter collaborations.
