Pascale Ouimet is a clinical nurse (inf., BSc) and clinical research coordinator at CHU Sainte-Justine. She oversees the day-to-day coordination of research projects in the NeuroConnect Lab, including ethics submissions, patient recruitment, clinical data management, and regulatory compliance.

Her role is essential to the smooth running of the lab's clinical studies and multicenter collaborations.
