David is a PhD student investigating deep brain stimulation (DBS) for disorders of consciousness and motor recovery following hemispherectomy. His doctoral work combines clinical outcome meta-analysis, advanced neuroimaging, and neuromodulation approaches to improve care for patients with acquired brain injury.

He co-authored a multicenter meta-analysis on central thalamic DBS published in the Journal of Neurosurgery (2025) and a review on motor recovery mechanisms following hemispherectomy in the Journal of Child Neurology (2026).
