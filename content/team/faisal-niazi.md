Dr. Niazi is a clinical research fellow in the NeuroConnect Lab specializing in surgical outcomes for drug-resistant epilepsy, VNS in children, hypothalamic hamartoma surgery, and emergency neurosurgery for refractory status epilepticus.

He has led several high-impact meta-analyses and systematic reviews, and is a key contributor to the CONNECTiVOS and CHILD-DBS international collaborations.
