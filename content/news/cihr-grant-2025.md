---
title: "Lab receives CIHR grant for pediatric epilepsy surgery outcomes study"
date: 2025-03-15
category: "Award"
summary: "We are proud to announce a 3-year CIHR operating grant to study long-term cognitive and seizure outcomes following epilepsy surgery in pediatric and adult populations."
---

The NeuroConnect Lab, led by Dr. Alexander Weil, has been awarded a 3-year operating grant from the Canadian Institutes of Health Research (CIHR) to conduct a prospective study on cognitive and quality-of-life outcomes after epilepsy surgery.

This study will follow children and adults for up to 5 years after surgery, combining comprehensive neuropsychological assessments, patient-reported outcome measures, and neuroimaging biomarkers to identify predictors of long-term success.

We are grateful to CIHR for their support of this important work, and to our patients and families who make this research possible.
