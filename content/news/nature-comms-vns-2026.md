A major output of the **CONNECTiVOS** consortium, co-led by our lab, has been published in **Nature Communications**. This study developed and validated a deep representation learning model trained on structural brain MRI to predict which children with drug-resistant epilepsy will respond to vagus nerve stimulation (VNS).

The model was trained and validated across 10+ North American pediatric epilepsy centers and significantly outperforms existing clinical prediction tools.

**Full citation:** Suresh H, Mithani K, Li V, ..., Weil AG, ..., Ibrahim GM. A deep representation learning model to predict response to vagus nerve stimulation. *Nature Communications*, 2026. [DOI: 10.1038/s41467-026-71555-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-71555-0)
