We are excited to share a new publication from our collaboration with the Bernhardt Lab (McGill), Bernasconi Lab (McGill), and international partners, published in **Nature Communications**.

The study, led by Ke Xie and colleagues, identified personalized biomarkers of multiscale functional alterations in temporal lobe epilepsy using large-scale connectome analyses. Our lab contributed clinical data and SEEG recordings from our patient cohort.

**Full citation:** Xie K, Sahlas E, Ngo A, Chen J, Arafat T, ..., Weil AG, ..., Bernhardt BC. Personalized biomarkers of multiscale functional alterations in temporal lobe epilepsy. *Nature Communications*, 16(1), 10145, 2025. [DOI: 10.1038/s41467-025-65042-1](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-65042-1)
