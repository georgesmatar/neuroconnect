Welcome to the new **NeuroConnect Lab** website! We have built this platform to share our research, introduce our team, and communicate our science to the broader community — from patients and families to scientists and clinicians worldwide.

We are also announcing the initiation of a new prospective study on **high-frequency oscillations (HFOs)** as biomarkers of epileptogenic tissue in drug-resistant focal epilepsy. This study uses SEEG recordings to characterize ripples and fast ripples in the seizure onset zone and surrounding tissue, with the goal of improving our surgical targeting strategy.
